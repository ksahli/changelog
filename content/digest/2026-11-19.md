- PHP shipped 8.5.13/8.4.28/8.3.37/8.2.36 fixing 18 flaws, including critical RCEs such as phpAfterShell (CVE-2026-126714); patch every PHP fleet now.
- Critical unauthenticated RCEs landed in NGINX ('nginx2root'), VMware vCenter (CVSS 9.8 auth bypass), Drupal core and Ruby's regex/string code; Ruby says proof-of-concept RCE exploits exist.
- CISA added actively exploited Synology DSM, BerriAI LiteLLM, Apache Axis and REDCap flaws to KEV, and a PRC-nexus actor is exploiting vSphere.
- PostgreSQL 19 and PHP 8.6.0 are out, along with ASP.NET Core in .NET 11; Lambda now allows 30-minute on-demand timeouts.
