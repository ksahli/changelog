---

- 1Password disclosed a breach where a hacked employee GitHub account was used to deploy malicious code to backend services, stealing customer names and emails (no vault data); expect targeted phishing.
- Drupal core RCE CVE-2026-132165 (Fail2Install, CVSS 9.8) is actively exploited and in CISA KEV with a Nov 27 federal deadline; patch Drupal sites now.
- Hex.pm (Elixir/Erlang registry) had six production servers compromised via a CVSS 10.0 RCE present since 2016; patched within ~15 minutes, but Elixir teams should watch for follow-up guidance.
- Attackers are chaining PHP2Shell and Fragnesia against internet-exposed PAN-OS management interfaces, and two GeoServer flaws are under active exploitation.
- Microsoft's November Windows updates are causing authentication issues; CISA added 10 KEVs including Danfoss and HPE flaws.
