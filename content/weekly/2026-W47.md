---

This was a heavy patch week for the runtime and web-server layer most teams sit on. nginx, PHP, Ruby, Ghostscript and GPGME all disclosed memory-corruption or code-execution bugs, several with public proofs of concept. At the same time, attackers exploited perimeter and virtualization infrastructure: vCenter as a months-long zero-day, Cisco Expressway within days of a PoC, PAN-OS, and BIG-IP. PostgreSQL 19 shipped two months late, largely because of the volume of AI-reported vulnerabilities, a sign of how much the discovery pipeline has sped up on both sides.

The other thread is AI as both attack tool and attack surface. Autonomous agents found and exploited a MONAI Label zero-day. Bell Canada attributes a breach to an agentic attacker. Inference providers leaked prompts for months without noticing. Watch for faster exploitation after PoCs land, more vendors ending support for legacy gear, and the growing push to sandbox agents, seen in gVisor moving to CNCF and Google's Agent Compute Engine.
