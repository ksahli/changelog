---

The week was dominated by a wave of critical RCE fixes in core web infrastructure. nginx, PHP, Ruby, Drupal and Ghostscript all shipped patches, and several of the bugs have named exploits and public proofs of concept. At the same time, edge and virtualization appliances were under active attack. VMware vCenter's auth bypass had been exploited as a zero-day since September, Cisco Expressway was hit shortly after a PoC was published, and F5 issued emergency BIG-IP patches. CISA's KEV additions tracked much of this activity. AI also showed up on the attacker's side: agents found zero-days in MONAI Label, and an attacker described as agentic breached Bell Canada.

On the build side, PostgreSQL 19 shipped late, which the team attributed largely to the volume of AI-reported vulnerabilities it had to handle. C++26 was finalized, and AWS delivered a run of Lambda, Aurora and observability changes. Keep an eye on three things. First, the AI supply chain as a data-exposure risk, after the OpenRouter provider compromise and the prompt canary study. Second, sandboxing for agents, with gVisor moving to CNCF and Google launching Agent Compute Engine. Third, whether the nginx, PHP and Ruby bugs move from PoC to mass exploitation in the coming days.
